Intisari Rumus SNI 2847:2019 (Beton)

• Ec = 4700 × √f'c (MPa) [Beton Normal]

• β1 = 0.85 - 0.05 × (f'c - 28)/7   (0.65 ≤ β1 ≤ 0.85)

• a = (Ast × fy) / (0.85 × f'c × b)

• φMn = φ × Ast × fy × (d - a/2)

• Vc = 0.17 × (1 + Nu/(14Ag)) × √f'c × b × d

Intisari Rumus SNI 1725:2016 (Jembatan)

• DLA = 0.40 (L ≤ 50m); DLA = 0.40 - 0.0025(L - 50) (50 < L < 90m)

• Mmax = (qu × L²)/8 + (Pu × L)/4

• Lendutan Izin Jembatan: δijin = L / 800

Intisari Rumus SNI 1726:2019 (Gempa Seismik)

• SDS = 2/3 × Fa × Ss  |  SD1 = 2/3 × Fv × S1

• Geser Dasar Seismik: V = Cs × W

• Fx = (wx × hx^k) / Σ(wi × hi^k) × V
